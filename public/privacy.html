Jardin Soleil

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 27, 2026.

Your garden data

Jardin Soleil does not require an account. Plants, zones, journal entries, tasks, saved photos, learning and game progress, recipes, and preferences are stored on your device. Jardin Soleil does not sell personal information or use garden records for advertising.

Online features

Weather requests send a city or approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo. When you choose Plant Finder photo identification, a selected photo and limited observation context are sent through Jardin Soleil’s secure function to Pl@ntNet. External Conservatory AI is optional and sends a question plus only the context categories enabled by the user. Plant Health photo analysis remains local unless an external service is explicitly configured and used.

Permissions

Camera or photo access is used only when you choose a photo. Location is optional and supports weather and identification observations. Microphone access is used only after starting voice logging. The rest of the app remains available if permission is denied.

Data control

The app’s Garden Settings can export, import, or clear local garden data. Uninstalling the app may permanently remove data that was not exported first.
